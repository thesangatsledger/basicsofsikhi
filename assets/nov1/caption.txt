CAPTION:

42 years ago this week, Sikhs were hunted in the streets of Delhi. Our community rebuilt itself on seva, on giving to each other without asking for anything back.

Seva runs on trust. So on November 1st, we're asking: who checks the people who speak for the Sangat?

ਹਕੁ ਪਰਾਇਆ ਨਾਨਕਾ ਉਸੁ ਸੂਅਰ ਉਸੁ ਗਾਇ ॥
Hak paraaiaa naanakaa us sooar us gaae.
"To take what rightfully belongs to another is like a Muslim eating pork, or a Hindu eating beef." (Guru Nanak Dev Ji, Ang 141)

That's the standard. Here's where three organizations stand against it:

🔴 KidsPlay Foundation: police have charged its founder with fraud and theft over $5,000, and say some charges are connected to the charity. He denies all allegations, and nothing has been proven in court. The foundation says its mission "transcends any one individual."

🟠 3HO / Yogi Bhajan: an independent investigation commissioned by 3HO's own organizations found it "more likely than not" that Yogi Bhajan sexually abused followers. Survivors describe seva used as unpaid labour.

🟢 Basics of Sikhi / Harman Singh: no charges, no findings. Harman Singh is CEO of Everythings 13 (US), Basics of Sikhi's US charity, and signed its 2024 and 2025 IRS returns. Those returns book $230,172 as "government grants" and show 2025 wages above donations. In Canada, 95¢ of every dollar spent in 2024 went to consultants the charity doesn't name, with no program spending reported since 2021. He has not commented. We'll publish his reply in full.

These are three very different cases. One is before the courts. One was investigated. One just hasn't answered. That's exactly why you ask early: trust should be checked before something goes wrong, not after.

Give generously. Then ask where it went.
