Got the balls to read where your Dasvandh goes? 👀

Most people never will. The charities behind Basics of Sikhi file their finances with three governments, and almost nobody reads them. We did. Every number in this post comes from their own filings.

🇨🇦 95¢ of every $1 the Canadian charity spent in 2024 went to consultants it doesn't name.
📈 In 2021, that was 7¢.
❓ Since 2021, it hasn't reported how much went to the actual work: the teaching, the videos, the outreach.
🇬🇧 The UK arm took in £623,030 last year. Its fundraising costs rose 83% in two years. Its income rose 6%.
🇺🇸 In the US, $230,172 of $230,176 raised in 2024 was booked as "government grants." No government is named.
🇺🇸 In 2025, the US arm paid more in wages ($130,794) than donors gave ($120,559).

When people asked about this online, the posts were deleted.

We're not saying anyone broke the law. We're asking the question every donor has a right to ask: is this where you thought your money went?
